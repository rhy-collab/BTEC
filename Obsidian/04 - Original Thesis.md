## The Vision

A global, AI-native, Pearson-accredited online BTEC school that delivers high-quality, affordable education to families worldwide — starting with Business Level 3.

## The Problem

| Problem | Scale |
|---------|-------|
| Private school fees | £15k–25k+/year |
| State school quality | Variable, overcrowded |
| Online school options | Limited, expensive, low quality |
| AI-native education | Doesn't exist yet |

## The Solution

An online BTEC school that is:
- **AI-native**: AI augments teaching, assessment, feedback, and personalisation
- **Teacher-led**: Qualified, experienced teachers provide human feedback
- **Affordable**: 80–90% less than private school
- **Global**: Any geography, any timezone, any pace
- **Recognised**: Pearson BTEC qualification, globally portable

## Key Stats

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Price | £2k–5k/year |
| Private school comparison | 80–90% cheaper |
| Student-teacher ratio | Small (AI handles scale) |
| Geography | Global |
| Recognition | Pearson BTEC (160+ countries) |
| UK university acceptance | 100+ universities |

## The Founder

- Experienced BTEC teacher
- 95%+ distinction rate among previous students
- Deep understanding of AI capabilities
- Direct experience with Pearson requirements

## The Market

| Segment | Size | Willingness to Pay |
|---------|------|-------------------|
| Expat families | Large, growing | High |
| Homeschoolers | Growing globally | Medium–High |
| School refusers | Increasing | Medium |
| Career changers | Large | Medium |
| International students | Very large | Medium–High |

## The Thesis

> "There is a global market of families who want a recognised British qualification for their children but cannot afford private school, are not well-served by local alternatives, or need more flexibility than traditional schools provide. An AI-native online BTEC school can serve this market at 80–90% lower cost while maintaining or exceeding quality."
