1. The Service

UK BTEC Online provides online Pearson Edexcel BTEC Business Level 3 qualification tuition. Students complete 4-5 written research reports over 2 years with support from UK-qualified teachers. The qualification is awarded by Pearson Edexcel, regulated by Ofqual.

2. Enrolment

Enrolment is confirmed upon completion of the enrolment form and receipt of the first payment. Students must be aged 16 or over. English proficiency at a level sufficient to complete written coursework is required.

3. Fees and Payment

Fees are as listed on our pricing page. Payment options include monthly, 6-monthly, annual, or full-course upfront. All prices are in GBP. Late payments may result in suspension of access to lessons and teacher support. Refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis within 14 days of initial enrolment.

4. Student Responsibilities

  • Complete and submit written reports according to the provided schedule
  • Attend live lessons or catch up with recordings
  • Maintain academic integrity — all work must be the student's own
  • Communicate with teachers professionally and promptly

5. Our Responsibilities

  • Provide UK-qualified teacher support throughout the programme
  • Deliver structured feedback on written reports
  • Conduct live lessons with recordings available for catch-up

6. Academic Integrity

All submitted work must be the student's own. Plagiarism, AI-generated submissions presented as original work, or any form of academic dishonesty will result in the report being rejected and may lead to dismissal from the programme. We follow Pearson Edexcel's academic integrity policies.
