Online BTEC Business · Level 3 · Age 16+

An alternative route to university—without putting everything on final exams.

Complete a two-year Pearson BTEC International Level 3 Extended Diploma in Business pathway from wherever you live. Learn through applied assignments, live teaching and clear feedback, with support to plan your next university or career step.

Explore your route →How BTEC works

The qualification is equivalent in size to three International A Levels. Official enrolment requires an approved Pearson centre or confirmed partner-centre route. University entry is always decided by the receiving institution.
