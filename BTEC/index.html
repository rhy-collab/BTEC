The key difference

A Level-sized study, assessed in a different way.

Many university routes ask for Level 3 qualifications. International A Levels usually concentrate achievement into subject examinations. The BTEC Extended Diploma covers a comparable volume of study through one substantial Business qualification.

International A Levels

Knowledge demonstrated in timed exams

  • Usually study three separate subjects
  • Learn broad syllabuses over two years
  • Write answers under timed examination conditions
  • Final grades depend heavily on exam performance
BTEC Business Extended Diploma

Knowledge demonstrated across 15 units

  • One coherent Business qualification
  • Equivalent in size to three International A Levels
  • Research, reports, plans, presentations and practical evidence
  • No traditional final written examinations
You do not “get three A Levels”.

You earn the Pearson BTEC International Level 3 Extended Diploma in Business. Pearson defines it as equivalent in size to three International A Levels: a statement about the scale and volume of the qualification. Recognition and entry grades still depend on the university and degree.

Why students choose BTEC

Show what you know over time.

If timed exams do not bring out your best, BTEC offers another serious Level 3 route. You still work hard, but you can plan, research, draft and develop evidence instead of recalling an entire syllabus in one high-pressure sitting.

03 · Progress

Aim beyond Pass

Move from accurate explanation to analysis, then to evidence-based evaluation and justified recommendations.

Support is structured, but grades are earned. Teaching and formative practice prepare you before assessment; formal feedback, resubmission and grading must follow Pearson and approved-centre rules. There is no unlimited rewriting until a Distinction.
