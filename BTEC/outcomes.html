From Kenya to Russell Group

A student from Kenya who had been studying A-levels found the exam model wasn't working. Transitioning to BTEC allowed him to focus on research and writing rather than memorisation. He achieved a Distinction and received offers from two Russell Group universities for Business and Economics.
