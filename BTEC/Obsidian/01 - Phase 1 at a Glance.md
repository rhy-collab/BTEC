# Phase 1: Market Validation at a Glance

**Status**: 🟢 Ready to Launch  
**Timeline**: 6 weeks (29 July – 9 September 2026)  
**Budget**: £500–£2,000  
**Goal**: Prove families will pay before building the full school.

See also: [[00 - BTEC Project Home]], [[02 - Phase 1 Full Strategy]]

---

## The Core Idea

Test demand through ads + calls across 6 international markets. Learn what families actually want, what they'll pay, and where you're strongest. Only if demand validates do you build the full school.

**Why**: Ads and calls cost £500–2,000. Building an academy costs £20,000+. Test first, build second.

---

## Six Test Markets

| Market | Expected Conversion | Price Sensitivity |
|--------|-------------------|-----------------|
| 🇦🇪 Dubai, UAE | **Highest** | Low (expat, high income) |
| 🇸🇬 Singapore | High | Low–Moderate |
| 🇭🇰 Hong Kong | High | Low |
| 🇲🇾 Kuala Lumpur | Moderate | Moderate |
| 🇰🇪 Nairobi | Moderate | High (but serious) |
| 🇬🇧 UK | Lowest | High (direct competition) |

**Bet**: International markets 2–3× stronger than UK.

## Quick Reference

- **Form questions**: 7 validation questions (source, geo, budget, pain point, etc.)
- **Call script**: 15 min, listening, log pain point + price tolerance + timeline + competition
- **Email sequence**: 4 emails over 14 days (welcome → validation → social proof → urgency)
- **Messaging**: DO say "95% distinctions" / "pursuing Pearson approval". DON'T say "accredited" / "guaranteed grades"
