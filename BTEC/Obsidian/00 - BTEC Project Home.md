## 🎯 The Big Picture

Build a **global, AI-native, Pearson-accredited online BTEC school** that delivers high-quality, affordable education to families worldwide — starting with **Business Level 3**.

### The Core Value Prop
- **Price**: £2k–£4.8k/year vs. £15k–£25k+ for private school
- **Teachers**: Qualified, experienced, human-led
- **Qualification**: Recognised, respected, globally portable
- **Flexibility**: AI-augmented, self-paced, any geography
- **Integrity**: Real work, real feedback, genuine assessment

### The Risk
Building an academy for a market that doesn't exist. **Phase 1 exists to prevent this.**

## 🚀 Phase 1: Market Validation (6 Weeks)

**Goal**: Prove families will pay before building the full school.  
**Budget**: £500–£2,000 (ads + calls)  
**Risk**: If demand doesn't validate, we pivot or kill. No sunk cost fallacy.

### Six Test Markets

| Market | Why | Expected Conversion |
|--------|-----|-------------------|
| 🇦🇪 **Dubai, UAE** | Expat hub, £15k–25k private school fees | Highest |
| 🇸🇬 **Singapore** | Expat, British influence, competitive | High |
| 🇭🇰 **Hong Kong** | School-choice culture, high willingness to pay | High |
| 🇲🇾 **Kuala Lumpur** | Expat, British-influenced, underexplored | Moderate |
| 🇰🇪 **Nairobi, Kenya** | British tradition, emerging middle class, underserved | Moderate |
| 🇬🇧 **UK** | Baseline, local competition | Lowest |

**Hypothesis**: International markets (Dubai, Singapore, HK) convert 2–3× stronger than UK.

### Pricing to Test

| Tier | Price | Positioning |
|------|-------|-------------|
| Founding | £2,000/year | Early supporter, input on course |
| Standard | £3,500/year | Most popular tier |
| Premium | £4,800/year | Intensive + 2 weekly 1-to-1s |

### Success Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Website visitors | 500–1,000 |
| Form submissions | 30–50 |
| Booked calls | 10–15 |
| Call show-up rate | 60%+ |
| Pilot interest | 5–10 families |

### Phase 2 Triggers (Move Forward Only When ALL Are True)

- [ ] 10+ serious conversations completed
- [ ] 5+ families indicating "I'd pilot this"
- [ ] Clear price consensus (families agree on range)
- [ ] Geographic winner identified (1 market 2–3× stronger)
- [ ] Repeating pain point across calls
- [ ] Pearson approval feasibility confirmed

## 📞 Consultation Call Script

**Duration**: 15 minutes  
**Tone**: Listening, not selling

### Questions
- "Tell me about your family and why you're exploring BTEC Online"
- "Are you currently in school, homeschooled, or exploring alternatives?"
- "What's drawing you to BTEC?"
- "What concerns do you have about online learning?"

### Show
- Sample lesson (screen share)
- How feedback works
- Course structure

### Test
- "We're testing pricing around £2–3.5k/year. Does that feel realistic?"
- "We're in market validation. You'd be an early supporter."

### Capture
- Pain point (cost, flexibility, school fit, university progression?)
- Price tolerance (flinch at £2k or ask about £5k?)
- Timeline (must start September or flexible?)
- Competition (comparing to what?)
- Likelihood to pilot (1–5)

## 🎓 Sample Materials (Build Week 1)

### Lesson 1: Recorded Video (8–10 mins)
- **Topic**: "What is a Business? Why Does It Matter?" (Unit 1)
- **Format**: You on camera, whiteboard, one key diagram
- **Upload**: YouTube (unlisted) or Vimeo, link from landing page

### Assignment Brief (1 page)
- **Task**: Analyse a simple real business
- **Output**: 300–400 word report
- **Criteria**: Clarity, accuracy, application

### Feedback Template
- **Sample**: "You identified three functions. Next: explain *why*. See criteria."
- **Purpose**: Shows quality of support

### Unit Checklist
- Watch lesson → Summarise
- Submit assignment
- Receive feedback
- Respond to feedback
- Revision session
- Assessment prep
- Final assessment

## 🎯 Messaging Rules

### DO Say
- "We're validating demand for this offer"
- "95% of my previous students achieved distinctions"
- "We're pursuing Pearson centre approval"

### DON'T Say
- "We're officially accredited" (yet)
- "Guaranteed university admission"
- "Guaranteed grades"
- "AI writes your work"
