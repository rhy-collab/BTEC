## Core Messaging

### Positioning Statement
> "An affordable, online route through BTEC Business — with qualified teachers, AI-powered support, and a globally recognised qualification."

### Key Messages
- **Price**: 80–90% less than private school
- **Quality**: Qualified teachers, structured curriculum
- **Flexibility**: Self-paced, any geography, AI-augmented
- **Integrity**: Real work, real feedback, genuine assessment
- **Recognition**: Pearson BTEC, globally portable

### Tone
Professional, warm, credible. Not "too good to be true." Not "we'll replace schools." Position as a **supplement and alternative** for families who need flexibility.

## Call Script Framework

**15 minutes. Listen > sell.**

Ask: Pain point, current situation, budget, timeline, competition  
Show: Sample lesson, feedback template, structure  
Test: Price tolerance, pilot interest  
Log: Everything

---

## Success Metrics

- 500–1,000 visitors
- 30–50 form submissions
- 10–15 calls
- 5–10 pilot interested
- Geographic winner identified
- Price consensus
- Clear pain point
