# Phase 1: Market Validation at a Glance

## What Happened

You decided to test demand before building the full school. Phase 1 proves families will pay, identifies the strongest geographies, and maps pain points. Only if Phase 1 succeeds do you move to Phase 2 (compliance, curriculum, operations).

This cuts risk dramatically: you spend £500-2,000 on ads and calls to learn what you need to know, instead of investing £20,000+ building something no one wants.

## How We're Doing It

**Timeline**: 6 weeks

**Workstreams**:
1. Landing page (done ✓)
2. Sample lesson (1 recorded lesson + 1 assignment + feedback template)
3. Paid ads in 6 markets (UK, Dubai, Singapore, Hong Kong, Nairobi, Kuala Lumpur)
4. Form with validation questions (geography, pain point, budget, timeline)
5. Consultation calls (15-min listening calls, not sales calls)
6. Central spreadsheet tracking everything

## Why We're Doing It

Ads and calls tell you in 6 weeks whether demand exists and where it's strongest. You learn pricing tolerance (£2k? £3.5k? £5k?), geographic winners (UK competitive, but Dubai/Singapore/HK stronger?), and real pain points (cost, flexibility, school refusal, university progression).

## Six Test Markets & Why

| Market | Why Test | Hypothesis |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| **UK** | Baseline, local competition | Moderate demand, lower price sensitivity |
| **Dubai** | Expat hub, high private-school fees (£15-25k) | Strong demand, high price tolerance |
| **Singapore** | Expat hub, British school influence, competitive | Strong demand, moderate price sensitivity |
| **Hong Kong** | Expat hub, intensive school-choice culture | Strong demand, willing to pay for credibility |
| **Nairobi** | British tradition, emerging middle class, underserved | Growing demand, price-sensitive but serious |
| **Kuala Lumpur** | Expat families, British-influenced schools | Untested market, likely responsive |

**Expected outcome**: International markets (Dubai, Singapore, HK) likely 2-3× stronger than UK. Nairobi may surprise on price sensitivity but serious intent.

## Landing Page: 95% Same Everywhere

### Constant Across All Markets
- Headline: "An Affordable Online Route Through BTEC Business"
- Proof: "95% of previous students achieved distinctions"
- Features: Teachers, structure, cost, qualifications, flexibility, AI support
- Pricing tiers: £2k (founding), £3.5k (standard), £4.8k (premium)

### Geographic Tweaks Only
- Currency: £ / AED / SGD / HKD / KES / MYR
- Proof point: "Trusted by expat families in [city]" or "Recognised by [country] universities"

**Why?** Test whether your core value prop works universally. If it doesn't, you learn the message or market is wrong before investing in curriculum.

## Form: Ask Validation Questions

Instead of just email capture, ask:
1. How did you hear? (source tracking)
2. Which qualification? (Business only or broader interest?)
3. Current situation? (In school, homeschooled, left school, working, expat?)
4. What matters most? (Cost, flexibility, qualification, support, multiple intakes?)
5. Where are you? (Geographic validation)
6. Budget range? (Price sensitivity)
7. Preferred next step? (Friction point—call now or need proof first?)

**Learning**: Every answer teaches you something. Geo answers validate markets. Budget answers set pricing. Pain point answers shape messaging for Phase 2.

## Pricing Test Strategy

### Don't lock one price. Test three.

| Tier | Price | Positioning | Payment |
|------|-------|-------------|---------|
| **Founding** | £2,000/year | Early supporter rate, input on course | Annual or £180/mo |
| **Standard** | £3,500/year | Full support (most popular) | Annual or £295/mo |
| **Premium** | £4,800/year | Intensive + 2 weekly 1-to-1s | Annual or £400/mo |

Create three landing page variants (or use UTM params) and track which converts best by geography and channel. **Don't assume lower price wins**—premium positioning often converts better with expat/international families.

### Key Metrics

## Sample Materials (Minimum Viable Proof)

You don't build the full curriculum yet. Build proof your teaching works.

### Lesson 1: Recorded Lesson (8-10 mins)
- Topic: "What is a Business? Why Does It Matter?" (Unit 1, Business Environment)
- Format: You on camera, whiteboard, one key diagram
- Embedded on landing page, sent via email

### Lesson 2: Assignment Brief (1 page)
- Task: Analyse a simple real business
- Output: 300-400 word report
- Shows exactly what learners have to do

### Lesson 3: Feedback Template (Example)
- Sample feedback: "You correctly identified three business functions. Next step: explain *why*. See criteria checklist."
- Shows quality of support

### Lesson 4: Unit Checklist (What's Involved)
- Watch lesson → Summarise
- Submit assignment
- Receive feedback
- Respond to feedback
- Revision session
- Assessment prep
- Final assessment

## Consultation Call Script (Listening, Not Selling)

**Duration**: 15 minutes

**What you ask**:
- "Tell me about your family and why you're exploring BTEC Online"
- "Are you currently in school, homeschooled, or exploring alternatives?"
- "What's drawing you to BTEC?"
- "What concerns do you have about online?"

**What you show**:
- Sample lesson (screen share)
- How feedback works
- Structure

**What you say**:
- "We're testing pricing around £2-3.5k/year. Does that feel realistic?"
- "We're in market validation phase. You'd be an early supporter."

**What you learn**:
- Pain point (cost, flexibility, school fit, university progression?)
- Price tolerance (flinch at £2k or ask about £5k?)
- Timeline (must start September or flexible?)
- Competition (comparing to local schools, international schools, tutors?)
- Seriousness (genuine interest or just curious?)

## Success Metrics (Phase 1)

### Targets (4-6 Weeks)
- 500-1,000 website visitors
- 30-50 form submissions
- 10-15 booked calls
- 60%+ show-up rate
- 5-10 families interested in pilot

### What Counts as "Proof"
✅ At least 3 families in 2 different geographies seriously interested  
✅ Consistent price feedback (e.g., "£2.5k feels right")  
✅ Clear pain point pattern (e.g., families paying £20k/year seeking alternatives)  
✅ Geographic winner (e.g., Dubai 2-3× stronger than UK)  
✅ Willingness to book calls (demand exists)  

### If You Don't Hit These
- Pivot messaging (maybe it's not cost, it's flexibility)
- Pivot geography (maybe international is the answer, not UK)
- Pivot target (maybe tutors, not families)
- **Kill before investing further**

---

## Phase 2 Triggers (When to Build the Full School)

You only move to Phase 2 when **all** of these are true:

1. ✅ 10+ serious conversations
2. ✅ 5+ families indicating "I'd pilot this"
3. ✅ Clear price-point commitment (families agree on price range)
4. ✅ Geographic winner identified (one market is 2-3× stronger)
5. ✅ Repeating pain point (same need mentioned across calls)
6. ✅ Pearson approval feasibility confirmed

## Messaging Rules (Phase 1)

### DO Say
- "We're validating demand for this offer"
- "95% of my previous students achieved distinctions"
- "We're pursuing Pearson centre approval"

### DON'T Say
- "We're officially accredited" (yet)
- "Guaranteed university admission"
- "Guaranteed grades"
- "AI writes your work"

## Key Insight: International First

**Hypothesis**: International families will convert faster and at higher prices than UK families, because private-school fees abroad are 2-3× higher and families actively seek alternatives.

**Test this immediately**: Compare cost per lead, booked call rate, and price sensitivity between UK ads and Dubai/Singapore/HK ads.

**Expected outcome**: Dubai, Singapore, Hong Kong likely 2-3× stronger than UK by week 4.
