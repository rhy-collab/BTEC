What’s Included in Every Plan

Every pricing plan gives you the same comprehensive support. You pay the same way, just on a schedule that works for your budget.

Full BTEC Qualification

You complete the official Pearson Edexcel BTEC Business Level 3 qualification — equivalent to A-levels. On completion you receive a recognised certificate from Pearson Edexcel, regulated by Ofqual.

UK-Qualified Teacher Support

You get a dedicated UK-qualified teacher throughout your course. They guide every report, answer questions, and provide one-to-one feedback to help you achieve a Distinction.

Weekly Live Lessons

Attend weekly live online lessons where your teacher walks through assignment criteria, discusses business concepts, and answers your questions. Every lesson is recorded for catch-up.

Written Report Guidance

Each of the 4-5 written reports comes with clear assignment briefs, marking criteria walkthroughs, and structured feedback cycles. You know exactly what’s needed before you submit.

University Progression Support

We help you apply to universities worldwide. From writing references and managing equivalency to liaising with admissions teams — we stay with you until you’re accepted.

Recorded Sessions & Feedback

All lessons are recorded and available on-demand. You receive detailed written feedback on every draft and final submission, with clear guidance on how to improve and achieve a Distinction.

Fees — Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about pricing, payments, and plans.

Can I switch plans after I enrol?+
Yes. You can upgrade or switch plans at any time. If you start on Monthly and later decide to switch to Annual, we’ll pro-rate your payments so you only pay the difference. Contact us and we’ll handle the switch for you.
Is there a sibling or family discount?+
Yes, we offer a 10% discount for siblings enrolling together. If you’re enrolling two or more students from the same family, each additional enrolment receives a 10% reduction on their chosen plan. Contact us for details.
What is your refund policy?+
We offer a 14-day satisfaction guarantee. If within the first 14 days of enrolment you decide the programme isn’t right for you, we provide a full refund with no questions asked. After the 14-day period, refunds are considered on a case-by-case basis.
Are there any additional costs?+
No. The price you see is the price you pay. Your tuition covers: the full Pearson Edexcel BTEC qualification, all teacher support, weekly live lessons, written report guidance, feedback cycles, and university progression support. There are no registration fees, admin charges, or surprise costs.
Do you offer a free trial?+
Yes. We offer a free taster lesson with Rhys before you commit to anything. You get a real lesson, real teaching, and a real feel for how our programme works — with no obligation. Book it through our consultation page.
When do I pay?+
Payment is due at the start of your chosen billing cycle. Monthly payments are taken on the same date each month. If you choose 6-Monthly, Annual, or Full Course, the full amount is due when your enrolment is confirmed. We’ll send you an invoice and payment instructions before any payment is taken.
Can I pay from outside the UK?+
Absolutely. We accept international payments via bank transfer, debit/credit card, and PayPal. All prices are in GBP but your payment provider will handle the currency conversion. Many of our students pay from outside the UK with no issues.
