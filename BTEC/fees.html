A lower-cost online option

Designed to avoid the cost of full-time physical attendance.

Online teaching can cost substantially less than attending a private or international school because it does not include a campus, daily supervision, transport, facilities or a full school timetable.

Compare like with like

The £899 figure is a teaching-support price for this BTEC pathway. A physical school fee may cover many additional subjects, pastoral care, activities, facilities and official administration. Families should compare the complete service and all third-party qualification charges—not just the headline number.
