What Is BTEC Level 3 Business?

The Pearson Edexcel BTEC Level 3 National Extended Certificate in Business is a one-year (or two-year) qualification that is equivalent to one A-level. It carries the same UCAS tariff points as a full A-level and is accepted by universities across the UK and worldwide.

Over the course of the programme, you will complete 4–5 written reports instead of sitting timed examinations. Each report is a structured research project guided by your UK-qualified teacher, with detailed feedback at every stage.

With a 95% Distinction rate, our students consistently achieve the highest grade — equivalent to an A* at A-level — opening doors to Russell Group universities and competitive degree programmes.

Course Units

The BTEC Business Level 3 qualification is made up of six units. Each unit is assessed through a written report submitted to your teacher for feedback and grading.

1

Exploring Business

Coursework Report

Explore the features of different business organisations and understand what makes them successful. Investigate how businesses are structured, their aims and objectives, and the external factors that influence them.

📝 Assessment: Written Report 📊 Credits: 10
2

Developing a Marketing Campaign

Coursework Report

Learn how to plan and develop a marketing campaign for a real or hypothetical business. Research target markets, analyse the marketing mix, and create a justified campaign proposal with budget and evaluation.

📝 Assessment: Written Report 📊 Credits: 10
3

Personal and Business Finance

Coursework Report

Build a strong foundation in financial literacy. Understand personal finance, budgeting, savings, and investments. Explore business finance including profit, cash flow, break-even analysis, and financial statements.

📝 Assessment: Written Report 📊 Credits: 10
4

Managing an Event

Coursework Report

Plan, coordinate, and evaluate a real or simulated business event. Develop project management skills including budgeting, scheduling, risk management, and stakeholder communication from concept to review.

📝 Assessment: Written Report 📊 Credits: 10
5

International Business

Coursework Report

Investigate how businesses operate in a global context. Explore international trade, cultural differences, global marketing strategies, and the challenges and opportunities of expanding into overseas markets.

📝 Assessment: Written Report 📊 Credits: 10
6

Principles of Management

Coursework Report

Examine the key principles of business management including leadership styles, organisational culture, human resource management, and operational decision-making in modern business environments.

📝 Assessment: Written Report 📊 Credits: 10

How Assessment Works

Unlike traditional A-levels, BTEC is assessed entirely through coursework. No exams. No timed papers. Just structured written reports with clear criteria and feedback.

1

You Receive the Brief

Each unit begins with a detailed assignment brief from Pearson Edexcel. Your teacher walks you through the criteria, explaining exactly what you need to cover to achieve a Pass, Merit, or Distinction.

2

Research & Plan

You conduct independent research using provided resources, textbooks, and online materials. Your teacher guides you on research methods, sources, and how to structure your findings effectively.

3

Draft Your Report

You write your first draft. This is where you get our most valuable support — detailed written and verbal feedback from your UK-qualified teacher before you submit anything final.

4

Feedback & Improve

Your teacher returns your draft with structured notes: what's working, what needs more evidence, and how to reach the next grade boundary. You revise and resubmit for further feedback if needed.

5

Final Submission

Once you and your teacher are confident the work meets the criteria, you submit your final report. This is assessed internally and moderated by Pearson Edexcel to ensure national standards.

6

Receive Your Grade

Your report is graded as Pass, Merit, or Distinction. Over the course, your unit grades combine to give you an overall grade for the qualification, which translates into UCAS tariff points.

Resubmission Policy: You have the opportunity to improve your work based on teacher feedback before final submission. Unlike exams, where one performance determines your grade, BTEC assessment gives you the chance to refine and improve. Our 95% Distinction rate reflects the effectiveness of this feedback-cycle approach.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the BTEC Business curriculum, assessment, and how we support your learning.

How long does the BTEC Business programme take?+
The Pearson Edexcel BTEC Level 3 National Extended Certificate in Business is designed to be completed over one to two years. Most of our students complete the programme over two academic years, working at a comfortable pace alongside other commitments. The 2-year route allows plenty of time for in-depth research, feedback cycles, and achieving the highest grades.
What units form the BTEC Business qualification?+
The qualification consists of six units: Exploring Business, Developing a Marketing Campaign, Personal and Business Finance, Managing an Event, International Business, and Principles of Management. Each unit is assessed through a written coursework report. There are no exams. Your teacher guides you through every unit with structured support and detailed feedback.
What kind of support will I receive throughout the course?+
You receive full support from your UK-qualified teacher throughout the programme. This includes weekly live lessons (always recorded for catch-up), detailed assignment walkthroughs, structured written feedback on every draft, one-to-one support sessions, and full university progression guidance — from personal statements to university applications and references.
Can I study at my own pace?+
Yes. One of the biggest advantages of BTEC is the flexible, self-paced structure. While we provide a recommended timeline for each unit, you can work ahead if you're motivated or take extra time on topics you find challenging. The feedback-cycle model means you're never rushed — you submit drafts, receive feedback, improve, and only submit the final version when you and your teacher are confident it meets the criteria.
What resources and materials do I get?+
You receive everything you need to succeed: full access to the Pearson Edexcel BTEC specification and course materials, assignment briefs for each unit with clear grading criteria, curated reading lists and research guides, recorded lesson libraries you can revisit anytime, template frameworks for each written report, and one-to-one feedback sessions with your teacher. All you need is a laptop and an internet connection.
