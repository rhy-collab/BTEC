Curriculum and study plan

Your 15-unit Business pathway, mapped across 20 months.

The Extended Diploma contains six mandatory units and nine optional units, totalling 1,080 guided learning hours. This page shows the proposed full-time route; your final timetable and optional-unit combination must be agreed with the approved centre.

When can you start?

One main intake, with planned flexible entry points.

Pearson defines the qualification, but each approved centre controls its delivery calendar. These are UK BTEC Online’s planned study windows, not universal Pearson dates.

Main intake

September

Begin at Unit 1 and follow the standard September-to-June academic rhythm over two years.

Planned entry

February

Join with an individual onboarding and catch-up plan, subject to teaching capacity and registration arrangements.

Limited entry

April

A later start may be possible where the unit sequence and assessment calendar can still be completed properly.

Our aim is to offer a supported start within the next three months where a suitable intake is available. Rolling entry is never automatic: your written plan must confirm the actual start date, catch-up requirements, approved-centre registration and expected completion date.

See what you study and when.

This is an illustrative delivery plan, not a registered assessment calendar. Optional units, overlaps, evidence formats and deadlines may change after approved-centre review or to meet a learner’s destination requirements.

What will you submit?

Different units produce different evidence.

Business reports

Research, compare, analyse and evaluate real organisations or decisions.

Plans and campaigns

Turn evidence into a structured event, marketing or improvement proposal.

Presentations

Communicate recommendations clearly where the authorised brief requires it.

Practical evidence

Document activity such as event management or work experience.

Pearson does not prescribe one universal 8,000- or 10,000-word requirement. The evidence and workload come from each authorised unit brief.
