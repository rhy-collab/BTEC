International study and visa guidance

Your qualification can support your application. It does not decide your visa.

A completed qualification, transcript and clear progression plan may form part of an international study application. Admission, equivalency and immigration are separate decisions made by the relevant institutions and authorities.

Keep three decisions separate

Recognition is not one universal approval.

A positive answer at one stage does not automatically settle the next stage. Build your plan in the right order and keep written evidence.

01 · Academic

University admission

The institution decides whether your exact qualification, units and grades meet its course requirements.

02 · Recognition

Government equivalency

A ministry or education authority may operate a separate recognition or equivalency process.

03 · Immigration

Visa or study permit

The immigration authority applies its own rules on admission evidence, finances, identity, language and other requirements.

Documents to plan for

Build a complete, truthful evidence file.

The exact list depends on your destination. Your receiving university and official immigration guidance should determine what you prepare.

  • Formal offer or required sponsorship document from the receiving institution
  • Final qualification certificate and approved-centre transcript when issued
  • Unit information or equivalency evidence if requested
  • Financial, identity and accommodation evidence required by the destination
  • English-language evidence where the university or authority requires it
  • A clear explanation of why the course fits your education and career plans
