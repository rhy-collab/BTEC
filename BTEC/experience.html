Step 04

Feedback with integrity

Support is close. The submitted work remains yours.

Before formal assessment, Rhys teaches the knowledge, models research and writing approaches, and helps you understand the criteria. Once work enters formal assessment, feedback, resubmission and retake rules are controlled by Pearson and the approved centre.

  • Teaching and examples before formal assessment
  • Clear deadlines and progress milestones
  • Independent learner work and authentication
  • One authorised resubmission only where the formal rules allow it

What Rhys can do

Teach, explain, demonstrate methods, answer permitted questions and support planning.

What Rhys cannot do

Write assessed work for you, promise a grade or bypass controlled-assessment rules.
