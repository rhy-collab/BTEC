How to Enrol

Your step-by-step guide to joining UK BTEC Online. From your first conversation to your first lesson — we make the process simple and clear.

Your 5-Step Enrolment Journey

Getting started with your Pearson Edexcel BTEC qualification is straightforward. Follow these five steps and you’ll be learning with us in no time.

Step 1: Free Consultation

Book a no-obligation call with Rhys. We’ll discuss your goals, answer your questions, and confirm whether the BTEC written-report pathway is right for you or your child. No pressure, no commitment — just honest advice.

Step 2: Choose Your Plan

Select the pricing option that works best for your budget — Monthly, 6-Monthly, Annual, or Full Course. The more you commit upfront, the more you save. Every plan includes the same full Pearson Edexcel BTEC qualification and teacher support.

Step 3: Complete Enrolment

Sign up via our secure enrolment form, submit your documents, and receive your welcome pack. We’ll send you everything you need — your student portal access, lesson schedule, and course materials — within 48 hours.

Step 4: Pearson Registration

We register you directly with Pearson Edexcel as an official learner. You’ll receive your unique candidate number and be entered into the Pearson system. We handle all the paperwork — you just focus on learning.

Step 5: Start Learning

Attend your first live lesson, meet your UK-qualified teacher, and begin working on your first written report. You’ll get your lesson schedule, assignment briefs, and a clear 2-year roadmap. The journey to your Distinction starts here.

Entry Requirements

The Pearson Edexcel BTEC Level 3 qualification is designed to be accessible to a wide range of learners. Here’s what you need to get started.

🎂

Age Requirement

You must be 16 years or older at the start of the course. There is no upper age limit — adult learners are welcome and have successfully completed our programme.

Minimum age: 16+. No maximum.
🌍

English Proficiency

You need a working proficiency in English. This means IELTS 5.5 (or equivalent) OR you can take our free English assessment to confirm your level. We assess reading, writing, and comprehension.

IELTS 5.5 / CEFR B2 / Equivalent assessment
📚

Prior Qualifications

No specific prior qualifications are required to enrol. Whether you’ve completed GCSEs, IGCSEs, high school, or are returning to study after a break, you can join the programme.

No formal prerequisites needed

What You’ll Need to Enrol

Before you enrol, please prepare the following documents and information. This ensures a smooth, fast registration process.

  • Passport or National ID — for identity verification and Pearson registration
  • Proof of Address — utility bill, bank statement, or government-issued document (last 3 months)
  • English Proficiency Evidence — IELTS 5.5 certificate OR we can arrange a free online assessment
  • Previous School Reports — optional but helpful to understand your academic background
  • Payment Method — bank transfer, debit/credit card, or PayPal details for your chosen plan
  • Parent/Guardian Consent — required if the student is under 18 at the time of enrolment
  • Access to a Computer & Internet — stable connection for live lessons and submitting reports

Quick Tip

Don’t worry if you’re missing something on this list. During your free consultation, Rhys will walk you through exactly what’s needed for your specific situation. We’re here to help, not to create barriers.

After You Enrol

Once your enrolment is complete, you’ll receive: access to your student portal, your lesson schedule, course materials and assignment briefs, your Pearson candidate number, and a welcome guide with everything you need to know.
