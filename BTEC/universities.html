Where Can a BTEC Take You?

A Pearson Edexcel BTEC qualification is your passport to universities worldwide. With a Distinction, you hold a qualification equivalent to an A* at A-level — accepted at Russell Group universities, top international institutions, and everything in between.

University Acceptance

A Pearson Edexcel BTEC Level 3 Extended Diploma is equivalent to three A-Levels and is accepted at universities across the UK and worldwide.

BTEC vs A-Levels: The UCAS Comparison

The BTEC Level 3 Extended Diploma is a full-time two-year qualification that carries the same UCAS tariff points as three A-Levels. Universities treat it exactly the same way when assessing applications.

  • BTEC Extended Diploma (Distinction) — 3 A-Levels equivalent, up to 168 UCAS tariff points (same as A*A*A*)
  • BTEC Diploma (Distinction) — 2 A-Levels equivalent, up to 112 UCAS tariff points
  • BTEC Extended Certificate (Distinction) — 1 A-Level equivalent, up to 56 UCAS tariff points
  • Accepted at all UK universities including all 24 Russell Group universities
  • Recognised by universities in Australia, Canada, UAE, USA, Europe and beyond
  • Regulated by Ofqual — the UK government's qualifications regulator
A* Equivalent

A Distinction in BTEC Business is the highest grade you can achieve, carrying exactly the same UCAS tariff points as an A* at A-Level. It is the standard universities look for when offering places on competitive degree programmes.

University Entry FAQ

Common questions about using your BTEC qualification to get into university.

Is a BTEC really accepted at Russell Group universities?+
Yes. All 24 Russell Group universities accept BTEC qualifications for entry onto degree programmes. Many have dedicated pages on their websites explaining their BTEC entry requirements. Universities including Birmingham, Leeds, Manchester, Nottingham, Warwick, Exeter, York, and Southampton actively recruit BTEC students. A Distinction carries the same UCAS tariff as an A* at A-level, making you competitive for the most popular courses.
How many UCAS points is a BTEC worth?+
A BTEC Level 3 Extended Diploma (the full two-year qualification) is worth up to 168 UCAS tariff points — the same as three A-Levels at A*A*A*. Even the smaller BTEC qualifications carry UCAS points: a Diploma (2 A-Level equivalent) up to 112 points, and an Extended Certificate (1 A-Level equivalent) up to 56 points. Your UCAS application automatically converts your BTEC grades into the correct tariff points.
Do I need A-Levels as well as a BTEC to get into university?+
No. The BTEC Extended Diploma is a full-time qualification equivalent to three A-Levels. Most universities accept a BTEC Extended Diploma on its own for entry onto a degree programme. Some competitive courses may ask for specific grades or additional requirements, but the BTEC is a standalone university-entry qualification. You do not need to take A-Levels alongside it.
Can I use a BTEC to study outside the UK?+
Yes. Pearson Edexcel BTEC qualifications are recognised internationally. Australian, Canadian, European, Middle Eastern, and many Asian universities accept BTEC for entry onto undergraduate degrees. The UK NARIC framework provides official equivalency statements that confirm BTEC is equivalent to A-level standard. We help you navigate the equivalency process for any country you are applying to.
What if my chosen university asks for specific A-Level subjects?+
Some specialised courses (e.g. Medicine, Engineering) may require specific A-Level subjects. However, the vast majority of business, management, economics, social science, and humanities degrees accept BTEC Business without additional requirements. We help you research entry requirements early so you know exactly which courses are open to you with your BTEC qualification.
Do I need to take IELTS if I studied BTEC in English?+
Many universities accept a BTEC studied in English as evidence of English language proficiency, meaning you may not need a separate IELTS score. We provide documentation confirming your course was delivered and assessed entirely in English by UK-qualified teachers, which most admissions teams accept. Requirements vary by university — we check this for you before you apply.
What support do you provide for the UCAS application?+
We provide full UCAS application support: helping you choose your five university choices based on your predicted grades and preferences, writing a detailed academic reference from your UK-qualified teacher, guiding your personal statement to highlight your BTEC work, converting your grades into UCAS tariff points, and managing your application timeline to meet all deadlines. Our students consistently receive offers from their first-choice universities.
