University planning

Know what your chosen course expects.

“Equivalent in size to three International A Levels” does not mean every university or degree treats the qualification identically. We check the exact course, qualification title, units and grade profile.

01 · Explore

Shortlist courses

Compare subject content, location, cost, entry grades and graduate outcomes.

02 · Confirm

Contact admissions

Request written confirmation that the exact BTEC International qualification and unit profile are accepted.

03 · Prepare

Build your evidence

Organise grades, references, personal statements, portfolios and any language evidence required.

04 · Apply

Track every step

Keep deadlines, document requests, offers and conditions visible in one application plan.

The university makes the final admissions decision. Requirements can differ by course and can change between application cycles, so written confirmation is essential.

Career guidance

Business can lead in more than one direction.

Use the course to discover where your strengths fit: university, employment, an apprenticeship, entrepreneurship or further professional training.

  • Identify roles connected to marketing, finance, operations, HR and enterprise
  • Match optional units to interests and intended progression
  • Develop a CV, application profile and interview examples
  • Create a primary plan and a sensible alternative

Degree routes

Business, management, marketing, finance, HR, entrepreneurship and related programmes.

Employment routes

Entry-level roles where research, communication, customer and organisational skills matter.

Apprenticeships

Combine structured employment with further learning where appropriate opportunities exist.

Enterprise

Use planning, finance and marketing knowledge to test a business idea responsibly.

International applications

Confirm recognition before you commit.

For international applications, the university, education ministry or equivalency authority may request a transcript, unit list, certificate, centre confirmation or separate equivalency process.
