BTEC explained

A Level 3 route built around applied work, not final written exams.

The Pearson BTEC International Level 3 Extended Diploma in Business is a two-year qualification equivalent in size to three International A Levels. It can support university or employment progression when the receiving institution accepts the exact qualification and grade profile.

BTEC and A Levels

Two serious routes. Different ways to demonstrate learning.

Choose according to how you learn, the subjects you want and the entry requirements of your intended university course.

QuestionBTEC International BusinessTraditional A Levels
How do you show learning?Applied assignments, research, business analysis, plans, practical work and presentations.Mostly timed subject examinations, sometimes with coursework depending on the subject.
Are there exams?No traditional final written exams in this specification. Three mandatory units use Pearson Set Assignments and some tasks may use controlled conditions.Final written examinations are normally central to the qualification.
How long?Two years for the 1,080-GLH Extended Diploma.Normally two years.
What is the size?Equivalent in size to three International A Levels.Usually three separate subjects.
Can it lead to university?Yes, where the university accepts the exact BTEC title, units and grade profile.Yes, subject to course-specific subjects and grades.

“Equivalent in size” does not mean identical or universally interchangeable. Always obtain written confirmation from the university, ministry or equivalency authority relevant to your destination.

How assessment works

No traditional final written exams.

You complete internally assessed work and Pearson Set Assignments. Depending on the unit, evidence may include a business report, campaign plan, financial analysis, event evidence, presentation or justified recommendation.

  • Clear learning aims and assessment criteria
  • Pass, Merit and Distinction standards
  • Three Pearson Set Assignment mandatory units
  • Some set tasks may have controlled conditions
  • Formal resubmission only where authorised under Pearson rules
