About This Lesson

What the Taster Covers

This free session is built around Unit 1: Exploring Business — the foundational module of the Pearson BTEC Level 3 Business qualification. You'll get a genuine feel for how lessons run, how content is explained, and how you'll build your written assignments.

We'll cover the different types of business ownership (sole trader, partnership, limited company) and explore real-world examples so you can see how theory connects to practice.

  • Business types explained — sole traders, partnerships, and Ltd companies
  • Real-world case studies — apply theory to familiar brands
  • Assignment walkthrough — see how a BTEC report is structured
