Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about our BTEC programme.

How do I enrol?+
Enrolling is simple. Book a free consultation with Rhys first to confirm the BTEC pathway is right for you. After that, we send you the enrolment forms and you choose your payment plan — monthly, 6-monthly, annual, or full course. Once enrolled, you receive immediate access to your learning materials and are assigned your UK-qualified teacher.
Do I need prior qualifications?+
No formal prior qualifications are required to start the Pearson Edexcel BTEC Business Level 3 qualification. However, you should have a reasonable standard of English (equivalent to IELTS 5.5 or above) as the course involves writing long reports. If you're unsure whether your English level is sufficient, your free consultation with Rhys will help determine this.
Can I study from anywhere?+
Yes. Our UK online school is designed for international learners. You study from anywhere in the world with an internet connection. We have students from over 15 countries including the UAE, UK, Hong Kong, Singapore, Kenya, Malaysia, Nigeria, and beyond. All lessons are live online and recorded for catch-up across time zones.
What support will I get?+
You get a dedicated UK-qualified teacher assigned to you from day one. Support includes: weekly live lessons with recordings, detailed written feedback on every report draft, structured assignment walkthroughs, one-to-one progress reviews, university application assistance, and direct email/WhatsApp access to your teacher. You are never left to figure it out alone.
How quickly can I start?+
You can start within days of your consultation. Once enrolled, your teacher is assigned immediately and you get access to the course platform, your first assignment brief, and scheduled live lessons. There are no fixed term dates — you begin when you're ready, and your two-year programme runs from your start date.
Is there a trial period?+
Yes. Your free consultation with Rhys is effectively a trial — you get to ask every question, see the course structure, and decide if the BTEC written-report pathway fits your learning style before committing. There is no obligation to enrol after the consultation. If you do enrol on the monthly plan, you can cancel at any time with no notice period.
