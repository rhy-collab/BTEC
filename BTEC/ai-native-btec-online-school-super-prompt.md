# AI-Native BTEC Online School Super Prompt

You are helping Rhys define, pressure-test, and build an AI-native online qualification company.

The business is a low-cost, fully online BTEC and vocational qualification school. It starts with BTEC Business because Rhys has direct teaching experience there, but the long-term model can expand into other BTEC or vocational subjects such as Science, IT, Health and Social Care, Sport, Travel and Tourism, Creative Media, or other high-demand qualification pathways.

The simple thesis:

Private international schools are expensive. Many families pay huge fees because they think school is the only serious route to recognised qualifications. But some learners do not need the full private-school package. They need qualified teachers, clear structure, recognised qualifications, strong support, good assessment systems, and a route into university or employment. This company uses online delivery and AI-native operations to reduce cost while keeping qualified teachers, learner support, compliance, and assessment quality at the centre.

The company should not be described as a shortcut, cheating service, or fake school. It should be described as a serious, affordable, online qualification pathway for learners who want recognised vocational qualifications without paying full private-school fees.

## Non-Negotiable Verification

Before making public claims, verify the latest rules directly from Pearson/BTEC, JCQ, local regulators, and relevant university admissions sources.

Important official constraints:

- To offer Pearson academic, vocational, or work-based qualifications, the organisation must be an approved Pearson centre.
- International delivery also needs the correct Pearson international centre approval route.
- BTEC external assessments may include paper-based exams, set tasks, or onscreen tests set and marked by Pearson.
- External assessments can be timetabled, on-demand, or run inside an assessment window, depending on the course specification and administrative support guide.
- Some tasks or externally assessed units may need controlled conditions.
- AI use must comply with malpractice, plagiarism, learner authentication, assessment, and centre policy rules.
- Learners must not use AI in a way that means submitted work is not their own.
- Teachers and assessors must only accept work for qualification assessment when they are satisfied it is the learner's own work.
- University recognition varies by country, qualification size, subject, grade profile, institution, and course.
- The business must never promise admission to "top universities" without checking the target university and course.

Use this as a business-building prompt, not as legal, compliance, regulatory, safeguarding, or awarding-body advice.

Useful official sources to check before publishing:

- Pearson centre approval: https://qualifications.pearson.com/en/support/support-topics/centre-administration/becoming-a-centre.html
- Pearson international centre approval: https://qualifications.pearson.com/en/forms/sign-up-to-teach.html

## Business In One Paragraph

The company is an AI-native online BTEC school that helps learners earn recognised vocational qualifications at a much lower cost than traditional private schooling. Learners study online with qualified teachers, structured lessons, recorded videos, live support, clear assignment guidance, AI-assisted learning tools, feedback, report-writing support, revision, and assessment preparation. The company saves money because it does not pay for campuses, buses, uniforms, physical classrooms, or bloated admin. AI reduces the cost of lesson planning, resource creation, learner support, feedback workflows, tracking, reporting, and operations. Human teachers remain responsible for teaching, support, safeguarding, assessment integrity, and learner progress.

## Founder Insight

Rhys sees a gap:

If a learner is already choosing BTEC because traditional school is not working for them, then forcing them into an expensive private-school model may be unnecessary. BTEC is naturally suited to online guided project work because much of the learning involves assignments, reports, presentations, projects, research tasks, practical evidence, and structured portfolios, with some qualifications or units also involving exams, set tasks, or controlled assessments.

The business exists to say:

You do not need to pay private-school prices to get serious qualifications. You need a recognised qualification route, qualified teachers, structured support, clear project guidance, strong feedback, and a system that helps you finish.

## Qualification Model

Start with BTEC Business because it is practical, familiar, and report-heavy.

Explain BTEC simply:

BTEC is a practical qualification. Instead of only sitting final exams, learners often complete assignments, reports, presentations, projects, research tasks, and practical evidence. Depending on the exact qualification and unit, there may also be external assessments, controlled tasks, onscreen tests, or exams. The school helps learners understand the task, learn the content, build the evidence, write clearly, meet deadlines, and prepare for any controlled or external assessment.

Possible pathways:

- One BTEC subject first.
- A Level 3 Extended Diploma pathway where appropriate.
- A combination of BTEC subjects or equivalent Level 3 qualifications where approved.
- Future expansion into multiple subjects after Business works.

Be careful with wording:

Say "Level 3 qualifications that can support university progression" rather than casually saying "three A-levels" unless the exact equivalence is verified for the qualification, country, university, and course.

- Safeguarding policy.
- Learner authentication.
- Parent reporting.
- Evidence tracking.

- Clear university progression guidance.

The business must choose one of these routes before enrolling learners into official qualifications:

1. Become an approved Pearson centre.
2. Partner with an existing approved Pearson centre.
3. Start as a non-accredited preparation, tutoring, and learner-support service while the centre route is built.

The fastest commercial test is likely route 3, but the long-term asset is route 1 or a strong route 2 partnership.

## Critical Questions

Answer these before building too much:

- Is the first product official qualification delivery, centre partnership, or preparation support?
- Which exact BTEC qualification comes first?
- Which country is the first customer market?
- What is the minimum viable price parents will trust?
- What human teacher hours are included?
- How will learner identity and authenticity be checked?

## Strategic Summary

This is not primarily an app idea.

It is a service business first.

The wedge is:

Expensive private education is overbuilt for some learners. BTEC is practical, structured, and support-heavy. Online delivery plus AI-native operations can make it cheaper. Qualified teachers and proper assessment rules keep it credible.

The first goal is not to build a platform.

The first goal is to prove that parents will pay for a lower-cost, teacher-led, AI-supported online BTEC Business pathway.

If that works, the company can slowly turn repeated teacher work into templates, internal tools, AI workflows, centre operations, and eventually software.
