Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about our UK online Pearson BTEC school.

Is BTEC the same as A-levels? Can I get into university with it?
Yes. Pearson BTEC Level 3 qualifications are equivalent to A-levels and are accepted by universities worldwide — including UK universities through UCAS, Australian, Canadian, US, and European institutions. A Distinction at BTEC carries the same UCAS tariff points as an A* at A-level. It's a genuine, respected route into higher education.
I struggle with exams. Is BTEC better for me?
Absolutely. This is one of the main reasons learners choose BTEC. Instead of sitting timed exam papers in a hall, you complete 4-5 long written research reports over two years. Each report is broken down step-by-step, with clear guidance and feedback from your UK teacher before you submit. No exam anxiety, no blank-page panic — just structured, supported project work.
Is this a real UK qualification?
Yes. Pearson BTEC is a genuine UK qualification awarded by Pearson, the UK's largest awarding body. It's regulated by Ofqual (the UK government's qualifications regulator) and recognised by universities and employers globally. You earn the same qualification as UK students studying in British schools and colleges.
How does the 2-year written report structure work?
Over two years you complete 4-5 long written research reports — no exams, no timed papers. Each report is guided by your UK teacher with detailed walkthroughs, criteria checklists, and feedback cycles. You submit draft work, receive structured feedback, improve your report, and submit your final version. This builds your research, writing, and analytical skills — exactly what universities want to see.
Is 95% Distinction rate real?
Yes. 95% of our students achieve a Distinction — the highest grade, equivalent to an A* at A-level. This is because our model combines clear UK teaching, structured report guidance, feedback cycles, and progress tracking. You know exactly what's needed at every stage, and your teacher makes sure you get there.
Can I study from outside the UK?
Yes. Our UK online school is designed for international learners. You study live online with a UK-qualified teacher, around your own schedule. Families in Dubai, Singapore, Hong Kong, Kenya, Malaysia, and beyond use our school because it gives them a genuine UK qualification without paying full private-school fees. Universities worldwide accept Pearson BTEC for entry.
