Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about BTEC Online.

Is BTEC Business a real qualification?
Yes. BTEC is a respected, recognised vocational qualification awarded by Pearson. It's valued by universities and employers worldwide and is a legitimate alternative to traditional academic routes.
Will this help me get into university?
BTEC qualifications are recognised by many universities for course entry and progression. Recognition varies by university, course, and country, so we provide honest guidance during your consultation about your specific goals.
Can I really study online?
Yes. You access recorded lessons, live sessions, assignment guidance, and feedback through our online platform. You study when it suits you, with support always available.
How much of my work is AI-written?
None of your assessed submissions are AI-written. We use AI to help create clearer resources, faster feedback, and better support. Your work is always your own—that's fundamental to real learning and qualification integrity.
What if I fall behind?
We track your progress weekly and offer catch-up sessions, extended deadlines, and personalised support. The structure is designed to help you stay on track.
Is there a trial or money-back guarantee?
Yes. We offer a free consultation first, so you can understand exactly how it works before committing. We're confident in the model—you should feel confident too.
